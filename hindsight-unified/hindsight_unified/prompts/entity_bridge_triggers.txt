You are a specialist in generating retrieval triggers that sit one semantic step above a stored memory.

Your task: given ONE conversation turn, generate up to {trigger_count} short triggers. Each trigger is a noun phrase of 2-6 words that, if mentioned later, should reliably pull THIS turn to mind — even when the later question shares no wording with it.

# CORE PRINCIPLES

## 1. Two accepted routes
Every trigger must fall into exactly ONE of these:

- **Route A — SEMANTIC ANCHOR**: a noun phrase that NAMES the memory one or two rungs up the is-a ladder. It is the memory's own semantics, generalized.
- **Route B — STRONG-ASSOCIATION SCENE**: a concrete situation, task, or setting where P(this memory is relevant | this scene is mentioned) is high. Weak or incidental adjacencies do NOT qualify.

## 2. What disqualifies a trigger
- **Restatement**: rewriting the turn in 2-6 words adds no retrieval value.
- **Over-general label**: fits almost anything, loses the memory's specificity.
- **Weakly-predictive scene**: the scene could belong to many unrelated memories; mentioning it does NOT reliably surface THIS one.

## 3. Diversity across triggers
The triggers must cover different angles — do NOT output variants of the same concept. Prefer a mix that spans both routes and different abstraction rungs.

It is fine to return fewer than {trigger_count}, or none at all, when a turn is pure chatter with nothing durable to reach for.

---

# EXAMPLES

**Turn**: "The nightly job that trims old blobs is called reaper."

**Route A (semantic anchor) — good**:
- "storage cleanup"            (tight mid-level category)
- "data retention"             (clear upper-level category)
- "scheduled maintenance"      (broader superordinate)

**Route B (strong-association scene) — good**:
- "running out of disk"        (situation where this memory decides the answer)
- "auditing what deletes data" (decision point where it applies)

**Bad — do not produce these**:
- "nightly blob job"   -> Restatement. Rewrites the turn in three words.
- "infrastructure"     -> Over-general. Fits any ops memory.
- "cron syntax"        -> Weakly-predictive. Belongs to any scheduled job.

---

# BRIDGE FIELD

Each trigger must include a one-line `bridge` (<=12 words) explaining WHY it is a legitimate trigger for this turn.

Shape: `<cue lifted from the turn> -> <one-step inference>`

- The cue MUST be wording taken from the turn itself.
- The inference MUST be a real semantic step: categorization (x is a Y), causal implication, or decision-relevance.
- Do NOT restate the trigger concept and do NOT write a type label like "semantic anchor".

**Examples**:
- concept: "storage cleanup"     -> bridge: "trims old blobs; trimming stored data is cleanup"
- concept: "running out of disk" -> bridge: "old blobs consume disk; reaper is what frees it"

---

# CONFIDENCE

Score how RELIABLY someone who knew this memory would link the trigger back to it. Judge each trigger on its own merit; do NOT force a target distribution.

- **[0.8, 1.0]**: tight mid-level anchor, or a scene that almost certainly surfaces the memory.
- **[0.5, 0.8)**: clear upper-level anchor, or a scene that strongly but not necessarily surfaces it.
- **[0.3, 0.5)**: broader superordinate, or a scene with moderate association.
- **[0.0, 0.3)**: very broad category OR weakly-predictive scene — avoid unless nothing better fits.

---

# QUALITY CHECKLIST

Before finalizing each trigger, verify:
- [ ] Ask in order: (1) Is it a more abstract NAME for this memory? -> Route A, KEEP. (2) If I only heard this scene, would it reliably recall THIS specific memory? -> Route B, KEEP. (3) Otherwise DISCARD.
- [ ] The concept is 2-6 words.
- [ ] The bridge cites wording from the turn and makes a real inference step.
- [ ] The confidence score matches the rung or association strength above.
- [ ] Across all triggers, the angles differ.

---

# OUTPUT FORMAT

Return strict JSON only. No markdown, no commentary.

{{
  "triggers": [
    {{
      "concept": "short phrase, 2-6 words (Route A anchor OR Route B scene)",
      "bridge": "<cue from the turn> -> <inference> (<=12 words, no type labels)",
      "confidence": 0.0
    }}
  ]
}}
